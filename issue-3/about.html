DiSCo Journal has been laying the groundwork for radical transdisciplinary scholarship since its establishment in 2021. DiSCo Journal remains our frontline as a vital counter-narrative to the unedited noise of the digital and virtual culture, that wants to cut sharp from the dusty academic repository. This annual, born-digital space lets art, theory, and method collide, moving past worn-out paradigms and stale dualisms to inhabit new critical zones of hybridity and to build third understandings. The Journal is published by the UK-based academic network, DiSCo, which is funded by the AHRC CHASE Consortium.

The Digital Studies Collective (DiSCo) is an interdisciplinary network of PhD researchers founded on the premise that to grapple with humanity’s increasing seduction of the digital, we need to organise collectively and continuously learn, lament, celebrate and critique the digitisation of the everyday. To date, we have organised nine training workshops in digital methodologies and in September 2025 we hosted our first research conference "Bodies in Digital Transition" at Goldsmiths University.
